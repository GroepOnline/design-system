# Public editorial design delta

Use for product, company and explanatory public surfaces.

- One dominant visual idea per viewport. Supporting information stays subordinate.
- Use real product visuals, diagrams or evidence instead of decorative gradients.
- Typography may breathe more than operator UI, but controls still use canonical primitives.
- Product explanation follows problem -> product -> proof -> next action.
- Motion may support narrative sequencing but must stop and respect reduced motion.
