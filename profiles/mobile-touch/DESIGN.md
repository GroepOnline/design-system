# Mobile touch design delta

Use for phone-first or touch-dominant surfaces.

- 44px minimum coarse-pointer targets and safe-area aware layout.
- Secondary panes become deliberate sheets or full-screen routes, never accidental squeezed columns.
- Navigation uses explicit scroll strips, drawers or bottom actions; never uncontrolled wrapping.
- Preserve information priority before preserving desktop geometry.
- Motion distances stay short and direct.
